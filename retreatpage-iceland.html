Between Fire & Ice

Reykjavík & Vik, Iceland

Signature Collection

October 11-19, 2026 · with Jenniferlyn Chiemingo

About this retreat

There’s a rhythm to Iceland that feels otherworldly: shifting skies, endless horizons, and the quiet pulse of earth beneath your feet. This journey follows that rhythm. Over seven nights, we’ll travel across the island’s south coast, tracing a path from the iconic Blue Lagoon to the raw beauty of Vatnajökull Glacier.

Our days unfold at an unhurried pace: morning practices that ground you, scenic drives through lava fields and coastal villages, long pauses in the presence of waterfalls and volcanic sands. Each stop offers a different expression of Iceland’s character: geothermal warmth, ancient ice, and the simplicity of local life.

Evenings bring soft light, shared meals, and stillness. We’ll stay in carefully chosen hotels that blend comfort with proximity to nature, allowing the landscape to remain the true luxury. There’s time built in for exploration too, including Reykjavik’s quiet creativity, the surreal glow of the Golden Circle, and perhaps a glimpse of the Northern Lights.

This retreat is not about escape. It is about presence. About seeing a place that feels both wild and deliberate, and meeting yourself there, steady and awake to the world.

Rooms pricing

How to book

  1. Choose your room Every price is per person, in USD.
  2. Secure your space A $1,000 deposit per person holds your place. It comes off your total — it is not an extra.
  3. Pay the balance later The rest is due 90 days before the retreat.

Sharing a room? Book together with your roommate. If you don’t have one we’ll pair you when we can — and if nobody is available, we’ll move you to a private room.

Where you’ll be

Reykjavík for the first days, then south to Vik, where the beaches are black and the waterfalls come straight off the road. The photographs are from past retreats: the ice, the horses, and the sky on the nights it decides to perform.
