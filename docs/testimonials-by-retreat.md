# Testimonials by retreat page

Source: `Downloads/YAWW — Tripadvisor and Google Reviews.md` (real Tripadvisor + Google reviews). Quotes are verbatim, trimmed at sentence boundaries; only obvious typos or capitalisation were fixed. No ellipses, no hotel names other than Utopia.

## Read this first

**Locations with NO matching reviews in the file** (all pages below use general quotes): Camino de Santiago, France, Turkey, Argentina, Oaxaca.
**Locations with fewer than 3 same-location reviews** (filled with general quotes): Iceland (1), Santorini (1, a multi-destination reviewer), Basque Country (1, from the retreat teacher), Portugal (2 + 1 multi-destination), Sardinia (2).
**Locations with enough:** El Cuyo (13), Thailand (4), Montenegro (3), Morocco (3 positive + 1 multi-destination).

Notes
- Date convention: Tripadvisor shows the stay month. Google only shows relative dates as of 12 Oct 2026, so "c. 2025" etc. are approximations.
- The location tag in brackets is where the reviewer actually travelled. On pages where it does not match, show only name and source (not the location tag).
- Argentina: the Lori Gray review never names a country; "Tango and Polo lessons" suggests Argentina but verify with the client before showing.
- Oaxaca: no Oaxaca reviews; the Mexico quotes are from Tulum or an unnamed Mexican location.
- Basque Country: the only review is from the retreat's teacher (Genevieve M / Genevieve Morin, posted on both Tripadvisor and Google); consider labelling her "Retreat teacher".
- Montenegro: only 3 reviews exist, so both Montenegro pages share the same three (order differs).
- Excluded on purpose: the 2-star and 3-star reviews (Morocco noise, Google 2-star) and the Spanish machine-translated Portugal review.
