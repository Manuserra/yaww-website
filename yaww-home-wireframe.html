Pillar 1 · Inside the place

Beyond the retreat gate.

Most of our journeys move through two or more places. Mornings on the mat, afternoons with the people who live there: a shepherd's lunch in Sardinia, Day of the Dead with families in Oaxaca.

Curious, not extreme: every retreat shows its pace.
