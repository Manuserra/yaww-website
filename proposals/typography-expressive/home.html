The experience

Beyond the guidebook

Hunting for truffles in the woods of Croatia. Lunch with shepherds in the mountains of Sardinia. A meditation guided by a monk in Chiang Mai. Our journeys are shaped by the people we've come to know, the relationships we've built over the years, and a genuine respect for the places and communities that welcome us.

The way we gather

Pull up a chair

We follow the food. Local chefs, family-run kitchens, regional specialties, and long meals that have a way of turning strangers into friends. Eating locally is part of how we discover a destination and support the people who call it home.
