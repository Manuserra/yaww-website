Offset Your Carbon Footprint

The band has a "Learn more" link that goes nowhere, and no offsetting programme behind it. Three ways to handle it.

A · Remove it

Cleanest. Nothing on the page promises what the company does not do yet. Loses the gesture entirely.

(the section is simply not there: Gallery, then Additional info)

B · A quiet note before the footer

One centred line in small type above the footer. Keeps the sentiment visible; without a link it still reads like an unfinished thought.

Every trip has an environmental impact. If you would like to offset yours, you can contribute to climate projects through trusted organizations.

C · A question in Additional info Implemented

Where a reader who cares will look for it, in the same words, at no cost to anyone who does not. When a partner is chosen, the [missing data] line becomes the link.

Where can I find the full terms & conditions?+
Can I offset the carbon footprint of my trip?−

Every trip has an environmental impact. If you would like to offset your carbon footprint, you can contribute to climate projects through trusted organizations.
[missing data: the offsetting organization YAWW recommends, with its link]
