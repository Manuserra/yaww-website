Stone colour for secondary buttons and eyebrows

Christina dislikes the current stone-600 (#877C71). Four options moving it toward a warmer brown with some gold in it, all within the brand's family. Each one is shown as a secondary button at rest and on hover, as an eyebrow on the page background, and on the stone-200 and stone-300 module backgrounds (the "For teachers" band is stone-300).

One token changes: --stone-600 in css/base.css. Ratios are WCAG 2.1 contrast, worked out live on this page from the hex values. The eyebrow is 14px semibold, which counts as normal text, so it needs 4.5:1. On hover the label is the page stone on the colour, so the hover ratio equals the page ratio.

Today: Stone 600

#877C71 Current, for comparison

Grey-taupe with a green cast, which is what reads as "baby vomit" next to the cream. It also fails AA for small text on every background it sits on.

A · Warm taupe

#7E6B55

The smallest step: today's colour with the grey taken out and a little warmth added. Safe, but still more beige than gold, and as small text it misses AA on the stone-200 and stone-300 bands.

B · Toasted oak

#85684A

The most golden of the four, light and honeyed. Lovely as a fill on hover; as small text it only just passes on the page and misses on the stone-200 and stone-300 bands.

C · Bronze

#765C3A Implemented

Brown with clear gold in it, like aged brass or leather. Still reads as a sibling of the stone palette, but warm and expensive rather than dusty. The only gold-leaning option that passes AA for small text on all four backgrounds, including the stone-300 "For teachers" band, so one token works everywhere without exceptions.

D · Walnut

#6B5236

Deeper and quieter, closer to dark chocolate. Most contrast of the set and very calm, but the gold starts to disappear and next to the sea-blue primary the two buttons read as similar weight.
